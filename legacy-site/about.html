ABOUT LIANNE

A therapist who knows what it’s like to finally be seen.

Lianne Watkins is a trauma-informed, EMDR-trained therapist serving adults across California via telehealth. She has been on the other side of the couch. She knows from the inside what it costs to finally ask for help — and what becomes possible when the right approach meets the right person.

HER STORY

She doesn’t just hold space. She moves you forward.

Therapy changed Lianne’s own life. That’s not a tagline — it’s the reason she does this work. She sought her own therapy during a period of significant personal difficulty, found the right therapist and the right approach, and experienced what becomes possible when those two things align.

That experience sits underneath everything she does. She understands what it costs to finally make the call. She understands the relief when someone in the room actually sees you. And she understands the difference between a therapist who makes you feel heard and one who actually helps you change.

She is also a mother. That matters because parenting is one of the most profound sites where our own unresolved history shows up — in the moments we react bigger than the situation calls for, in the patterns we promised ourselves we wouldn’t repeat. She doesn’t understand this from textbooks. She understands it from life.

HER APPROACH

Warm enough to be safe. Direct enough to actually help.

Most therapists signal one or the other. Lianne signals both — and the combination is what makes the work possible.

She brings genuine warmth to every session. The kind of warmth that makes people feel, often for the first time, that someone in the room actually sees them — not the version they’ve been presenting to the world, but the real thing underneath. That safety is not incidental. It’s the prerequisite for everything else.

But she’s also direct. She won’t let you stay comfortable in the places that are keeping you stuck. She will tell you something true when it’s the thing you need to hear, not the thing you’re hoping to hear. Her clients describe the experience as feeling both completely safe and genuinely challenged — which is, not coincidentally, exactly the combination that produces change.

She is an intellectual who never stops learning. Her clients benefit from a therapist who is always at the frontier of what works — never coasting, always bringing the best of current research and method to every session.

WHY EMDR

She chose EMDR because it’s what actually works.

Lianne is EMDR-trained because she has seen — in her own life and in her clinical work — what happens when therapy reaches the level where trauma, anxiety, and painful patterns actually live.

Talk therapy is valuable. Understanding yourself matters. But for many people, understanding isn’t enough. The pattern keeps running even when you can describe it perfectly. EMDR reaches the level below words — the body, the nervous system, the part that fires before conscious thought — and helps the brain actually process and release what got stuck.

She also accepts 7 insurance plans and offers a sliding scale. Not because it’s easy to do — it isn’t — but because she believes cost should not be the reason someone can’t get help.

SUPERVISION & DISCLOSURE

Fully licensed and supervised — and transparent about what that means.

Lianne is a Registered Associate MFT and Associate PCC in California, working under the required supervision of Lovie Bucknell, LMFT #104497, through Kindred Heart Therapy Group. Supervision means her work is reviewed and supported by an experienced licensed clinician. Her supervisor is also bound by confidentiality. Your privacy is fully protected.
