The same fights. Different triggers. Same ending.

Family conflict is often about much more than the current argument. Families carry patterns, histories, and inherited ways of communicating that can make real resolution feel impossible. Individual therapy can be one of the most powerful entry points for changing a family system — by changing yourself within it.

Book Your Free 15-Min Call

Family conflict shows up in many forms.

Estrangement from a parent or sibling. A relationship with a family member that is consistently painful or draining. Adult children navigating difficult relationships with parents. Parents struggling with how their own childhood is showing up in how they parent.

Conflict with a spouse or partner that centers around family of origin issues — different values, different expectations, different wounds that are now in contact with each other.

Lianne works with individuals — not family systems — but her work often has significant ripple effects on the family relationships around her clients.

Processing the history that drives the pattern.

Most family conflict is driven by old material — experiences from childhood that created expectations, sensitivities, and ways of reacting that now run automatically in family interactions.

EMDR processes those specific experiences. When the charge around a particular dynamic reduces, you gain more space — more ability to choose your response rather than react automatically. That change in one person often shifts the entire system around them.

You can’t change the family. But you can change your part in it.

The free 15-minute call is where it starts. A real conversation about where you are and what’s possible.
