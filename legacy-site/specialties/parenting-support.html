When parenting triggers your own history.

Your child does something ordinary — has a meltdown, ignores you, says something that lands wrong — and your reaction is bigger than the moment deserves. You know it. You hate it. And you can’t seem to stop it.

This is often not about your child at all. It’s about what their behavior is touching in you — old wounds, unmet needs, fears, patterns from your own childhood that are now running the show in your parenting.

You can’t heal what you won’t look at. And looking at it — really looking at it — is where this work begins.

Processing your own history to parent more freely.

EMDR works with the specific experiences from your own childhood and history that are getting activated in your parenting. When those experiences are processed, the charge around certain triggers reduces — and you find yourself with more space between the trigger and your response.

This is individual therapy. Lianne works with you, not with your family system. But changing how you show up individually often has profound ripple effects on the family as a whole.

You don’t have to parent the way you were parented. That’s not a given — it’s a choice. And the work to make it real is some of the most meaningful there is.
