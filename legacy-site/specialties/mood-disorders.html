When your emotional baseline has shifted and you can’t seem to get back.

Mood disorders — including bipolar disorder, persistent depressive disorder, cyclothymia, and related conditions — affect how you feel, how you function, and how you relate to yourself and others. Trauma and unresolved emotional history often play a significant role.

Trauma-informed therapy as part of a broader care picture.

Lianne does not prescribe medication and does not work in isolation from other providers when medication is part of your treatment. She works with the psychological, relational, and emotional dimensions of mood disorders.

Many people with mood disorders carry a significant history of trauma, adverse childhood experiences, or long-term chronic stress that contributes to mood instability. EMDR addresses this layer — the root material that often amplifies or drives mood episodes.

If you’re already working with a psychiatrist or primary care provider for medication management, therapy with Lianne can be a meaningful addition to that care — not a replacement for it.

There’s more available to you than just management.

The free 15-minute call is the right place to start. Lianne will listen and tell you honestly what she can offer and how she can best support you.
