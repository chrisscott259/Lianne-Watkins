High-quality trauma therapy that’s actually accessible.

Most EMDR therapists in California charge $180–$250 per session out of pocket. Lianne accepts 7 insurance plans and offers a sliding scale for those without in-network coverage. This is a deliberate choice to make this work available to more people.

Accepted insurance plans.

Lianne is in-network with the following plans. If your insurance is listed, your sessions will be billed directly to your insurer at the contracted rate — you pay only your copay, coinsurance, or deductible.

Please contact Lianne directly to confirm which plans she is currently accepting, as her panel changes. The free 15-minute consultation call is a great time to check your benefits together.

No insurance? There are still options.

Lianne offers a limited number of reduced-fee spots on a sliding scale for clients who need them. She believes cost should not be the reason someone can’t get help. If the standard rate is a barrier, say so on the consultation call — she will work with you.

Have out-of-network benefits? You may still be covered.

If your insurance plan offers out-of-network mental health benefits, you may be able to get partial reimbursement for sessions — even if Lianne is not in your network. You pay the session fee, Lianne provides a monthly superbill, and you submit it to your insurance for reimbursement. Many clients with PPO plans get back 50–80% of their session costs this way.

Call your insurer and ask: “Do I have out-of-network mental health benefits for telehealth services? What is my out-of-network deductible and reimbursement rate?”

A few practical details.

Payment methods

Credit card, HSA and FSA cards accepted. Payment is collected at the time of service via the secure client portal.

Session length

Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Longer EMDR processing sessions may be scheduled when clinically appropriate.

Cancellation policy

24-hour notice required to cancel or reschedule without charge. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged the full session rate.

Telehealth setup

Sessions via HIPAA-compliant video. A smartphone, tablet, or computer with a camera and internet connection is all you need.

Ready to get started?

The consultation call is free, and it’s the right place to start — including checking on your insurance benefits together.
