WHAT HAPPENS ON THE CALL

Here’s exactly what the call looks like.

01

You talk, she listens.

Tell her what’s going on. You don’t need to summarize your whole history or explain it perfectly — just start with what brought you here. She’ll ask a few questions to understand your situation better.

02

She shares her honest take.

Lianne will tell you what she’s hearing, whether she thinks she can help, and what working together might look like. She won’t tell you what you want to hear — she’ll tell you what she actually thinks.

03

You decide.

No pressure. No pitch. If it feels like a good fit, you’ll schedule a first session. If not, she’ll tell you that too, and often point you toward something that might be a better match.

COMMON QUESTIONS

The things people wonder before they call.

Do I need to know what I want to work on?

No. You just need to know that something isn’t working. Lianne will help you figure out the rest.

What if I’ve tried therapy before and it didn’t help?

That’s one of the most common things she hears. It usually means you tried the wrong approach or the wrong therapist — not that therapy can’t help you.

Is this really free, with no strings attached?

Yes. No credit card, no automatic enrollment. The call is free because it’s the right way to start.

I’m nervous about starting.

Most people are. The nervousness doesn’t mean it’s the wrong decision. It often means it’s exactly the right one.

Do you take my insurance?

Lianne accepts 7 insurance plans and offers a sliding scale. You can see the full list on the Fees & Insurance page before the call.
